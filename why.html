Why it matters

Money slips between checkout and the bank every month. Nobody notices, because nobody reconciles three files by hand.

Shopify records what the customer paid, the gateway settles that amount minus fees, GST on those fees, refunds and chargebacks, and the bank shows one credit for a batch of days. The three files never agree, so a missing settlement or a refund that was never sent hides inside differences everyone expects.

How it works

Rules first, AI last

  1. Read the exports. Load the Shopify orders export, the gateway settlement report and the bank statement as CSV. Column names, Indian date formats and rupee amounts are normalized.
  2. Match on exact identifiers. Tie each payment to its order by payment reference or order receipt, and each settlement to its bank credit by UTR.
  3. Check every fee. Recompute the fee from your contract, by method and card network, plus 18% GST on the fee, and flag any payment charged more.
  4. Score the near misses. For records with no exact identifier, score candidates on amount, date, email, phone, name and product. Commit only when one candidate clearly leads.
  5. Send only the leftovers to AI. The records the scores could not separate go to the model with at most 5 candidates each. Its answer must be one of those candidates, or the record goes to a person.
  6. Explain every gap. Each problem comes back typed, sized in rupees, and written in plain language with the evidence behind it.

Why it can be trusted

Built to be checked

  • Zero wrong matches. Every stage on the sample links no record to the wrong one, and the tests fail if that changes.
  • Every line explains itself. Each match shows the rule, score or AI reason behind it, and each problem shows its evidence.
  • Your data stays in the browser. Parsing and matching run on your machine. Only ambiguous records go to the AI, and only when you ask.
  • Your fee contract, not ours. Edit the rates in the app and every fee check updates instantly.
